About

Vision is a new magazine about K-pop and K-hip hop culture. The word VISION symbolizes the magazine’s overview of contemporary culture.

Inside you will find vibrant photos and playful geometric shapes. This magazine is designed for fans of Korean culture and anyone else who might be interested.

Typography and color palette

Structure

The publication consists of 40 pages. The first and the last spreads are allocated for advertising, as it is often done in magazines (as they are most likely to be viewed by readers), the second spread contains the contents, the rest - articles on 2-3 spreads each.

Selected details

The magazine intended to have bold, punchy, slightly punk feeling.
